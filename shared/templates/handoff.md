# Handoff

Use this as a blank copy for an authorized handoff, never as a synchronized live session. Include reviewed facts only; omit secrets, raw chats, auth paths, and live state.

## Goal

State the concrete outcome.

## Current state

List verified facts and reviewed artifact references, plus what remains undone.

## Next action

Give one safe, concrete step and its intended owner.

## Constraints

Record relevant security, cost, publication, and scope boundaries.

## Verification

List checks actually run, observed results, and explicit evidence gaps.
